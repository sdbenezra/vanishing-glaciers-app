🏔️ HOW TO PLAY 🏔️

📍 Your Mission

Investigate Dr. Sarah Chen's laboratory to discover what happened to her. Examine evidence, talk to Dr. Patel, and learn about glacier science.

💬 Commands

  • look - Describe your surroundings
  • examine [object] - Investigate something closely
    Examples: "examine monitoring station", "examine workstation"
  • talk to maya - Speak with Dr. Patel
  • inventory - View evidence you've found
  • progress - Check investigation status

❓ Natural Questions

You can also ask questions naturally! Try:

  • "Why does this matter?"
  • "Tell me about glaciers"
  • "What happened to Dr. Chen?"
  • "How do you measure temperature?"

📋 Evidence Panel

Click the "📋 EVIDENCE LOG" header to show/hide the evidence tracker. It automatically opens when you discover new evidence!

🎯 Winning

To solve the mystery:

  • Find all 4 pieces of evidence
  • Examine each piece carefully
  • Talk to Dr. Patel about what you've found

💡 Tips

  • Examine everything in the lab
  • Read the climate science lessons in each evidence
  • Ask Dr. Patel questions about the research
  • There's no way to fail - explore freely!

🔊 Voice Features

Use voice input and output for a hands-free experience!

Voice Input (🎤 button):

  • Click to speak - Speak your command instead of typing
  • On-device recognition - Speech runs locally on your device
  • Privacy-first - No data sent to external servers
  • Visual feedback - Animated waves show when listening
  • Auto-submit - Stops after 1.5 seconds of silence
  • Fallback - Recognized text appears in input field for editing

Voice Output (🔊 button in top-right):

  • Dr. Patel's dialogue - Automatically spoken with character voice
  • Narrator voice - System messages, locations, and evidence descriptions
  • Mute/Unmute - Toggle all voice output on/off
  • Skip speech - New dialogue interrupts current speech
  • Speaking indicator - Visual feedback when voice is active

🤖 AI Mode (Optional)

Click the "🤖 AI OFF" button to enable AI-powered natural language understanding!

  • Better understanding - Type naturally, the AI figures out what you mean
  • Privacy-first - Runs locally in your browser, nothing sent to servers
  • First load - Downloads AI model (~2-3 GB, cached after first time)
  • Works offline - After loading, works without internet!
  • Fallback - Regular keyword mode works if AI isn't available

🤖 Enable AI Mode?

AI Mode downloads a small language model (~2-3 GB) to help understand your commands more naturally.

🔒 Your Privacy:
  • ✓ Runs 100% locally in your browser
  • ✓ Nothing is sent to external servers
  • ✓ Your commands stay on your device
  • ✓ Works offline after initial download

First Time Setup:

  • Downloads ~2-3 GB AI model (first time only)
  • Takes 2-5 minutes depending on connection
  • Cached for future sessions
  • Requires WebGPU-compatible browser

Benefits:

  • Better natural language understanding
  • More flexible command parsing
  • Understands context and complex queries

Note: The game works perfectly without AI mode using keyword matching. AI is completely optional!
