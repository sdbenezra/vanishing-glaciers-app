🏔️ HOW TO PLAY 🏔️

📍 Your Mission

Investigate Dr. Sarah Chen's laboratory to discover what happened to her. Examine evidence, talk to Dr. Patel, and learn about glacier science.

💬 Commands

  • look - Describe your surroundings
  • examine [object] - Investigate something closely
    Examples: "examine monitoring station", "examine workstation"
  • talk to maya - Speak with Dr. Patel
  • inventory - View evidence you've found
  • progress - Check investigation status

❓ Natural Questions

You can also ask questions naturally! Try:

  • "Why does this matter?"
  • "Tell me about glaciers"
  • "What happened to Dr. Chen?"
  • "How do you measure temperature?"

📋 Evidence Panel

Click the "📋 EVIDENCE LOG" header to show/hide the evidence tracker. It automatically opens when you discover new evidence!

🎯 Winning

To solve the mystery:

  • Find all 3 pieces of evidence
  • Examine each piece carefully
  • Talk to Dr. Patel about what you've found

💡 Tips

  • Examine everything in the lab
  • Read the climate science lessons in each evidence
  • Ask Dr. Patel questions about the research
  • There's no way to fail - explore freely!
